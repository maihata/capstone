## Project Background

Welcome & ようこそ！This interactive dashboard serves as the capstone for the Educational Data Science specialization completed as part of my Doctor of Education at the [University of Oregon](https://www.uoregon.edu/). My dissertation examines national and state-level patterns in Early Intervention (EI) exits, with particular attention to how race, ethnicity, and language intersect with policy and structural contexts to shape infants and toddlers’ special education service pathways.

Guided by QuantCrit (Castillo & Strunk, 2025), intersectionality (Crenshaw, 1989), and Social Determinants of Health (SDOH; U.S. Department of Health and Human Services, 2025) frameworks, this project was intentionally designed to move beyond simple gap comparisons. Rather than treating disparities as group-based deficits, the aim is to understand how inequities in EI exits reflect broader systemic conditions, including policy variation and access to resources across states.

To support this goal, the visualization approach shifts away from the more common presentation of odds ratios by race or ethnicity at the national level. Instead, the primary map (“Large Disparities across Categories”) displays disparity levels within each state, inviting users to explore how inequities take shape in local context. This system-centered framing highlights that disparities are not uniform across the U.S., but are shaped by state-specific systems, and decisions.

Exit categories are grouped into six analytically meaningful categories. Categories such as deceased and children continuing in Part C were excluded, as they are not directly related to disparity interpretation or are not coded consistently across states (for example, continuing in Part C is not used in Oregon, where I am based). Several closely related exit codes, including A child is no longer eligible for Part C prior to reaching age three, were collapsed into a single category to improve clarity and reduce analytic noise.

For readers interested in additional context, including the conceptual grounding and early design decisions for this project, please visit: <https://maihata.github.io/finalproj/posts/viz_0/>
