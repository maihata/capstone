## Welcome to the EI Exit Dashboard

Welcome & ようこそ！ This interactive dashboard examines how young children with developmental delays and/or disabilities move through Early Intervention (EI) and Early Childhood Special Education (ECSE), the special education services for children from birth to age five in the United States.

Rather than focusing only on group comparisons, this dashboard centers systems and policy contexts. The main map highlights the largest exit disparities within each state, inviting users to explore how patterns vary across local conditions and state-level decisions.
