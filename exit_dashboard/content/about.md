## Project Background

Welcome & ようこそ！This interactive dashboard is the capstone project for my Educational Data Science specialization within my Doctor of Education at the University of Oregon. It draws on my dissertation, which examines how infants and toddlers with developmental delay and/or disability exit Early Intervention (EI) services, with attention to how race, ethnicity, language, and policy contexts shape these pathways.

Rather than focusing on simple group comparisons, this project centers systems and policy contexts. The main map highlights where the largest exit disparities occur within each state, inviting users to explore how inequities vary by local conditions and state-level decisions.

For additional background on the conceptual grounding and early design decisions, please visit my full website: https://maihata.github.io/finalproj/posts/viz_0/
