## Welcome to the EI Exit Dashboard

Welcome & ようこそ！ This interactive dashboard examines how young children move through Early Intervention (EI) and Early Childhood Special Education (ECSE), special education services for children from birth to age five in the United States.

It centers structural conditions, policy contexts, and data practices rather than attributing disparities to individual children or families. The map highlights the largest exit disparities within each state, encouraging examination of how patterns are shaped by state decisions, system design, and local implementation contexts.
