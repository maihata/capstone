## About the EI Exit Dashboard

Welcome & ようこそ！ This dashboard is the capstone project for my Educational Data Science specialization within my Doctor of Education at the University of Oregon. It builds on my dissertation research examining how infants and toddlers with developmental delay and/or disability exit Early Intervention (EI) services across race, ethnicity, language, and policy contexts.

Rather than focusing only on group comparisons, this dashboard centers systems and policy contexts. The main map highlights the largest exit disparities within each state, inviting users to explore how patterns vary across local conditions and state-level decisions.
